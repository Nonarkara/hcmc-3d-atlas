## Palette colour conversions

The atlas appearance themes adapt Sanzo Wada plate 325 from
[Nonarkara/Palette](https://github.com/Nonarkara/palette), whose `data/colors.json`
credits [Matt DesLauriers' dictionary-of-colour-combinations](https://github.com/mattdesl/dictionary-of-colour-combinations)
and Dain M. Blodorn Kim's earlier compilation. Plate 325 contains Eugenia Red | B
`#da525d`, Naples Yellow `#fbe6a0`, Yellow Ocher `#e2b540`, and Deep Slate Green
`#112f2c`. Interface tones derived from these values are contrast adaptations.
They are digital conversions, not claims about printed ink or a historic
Vietnamese colour scheme. The Vietnamese material association is the atlas's
contemporary interpretation.
